## Musinsa ranking source

- Musinsa ranking requests use `automation/no1-background/ranking-policy.json` and `no1_background_ranking_feed` (CLI: `node automation/no1-background/read-ranking.mjs`). Read the current No1 Ranking Analysis category/period/gender/age settings; query section `all` and process numeric rank ascending. Do not substitute NEW, rising, keyword results or the general KREAM discovery feed.
- Ranking feed previews do not advance the general discovery cursor or consume a work order. The existing `cream_explore_start` remains the general KREAM runner, not a Musinsa ranking runner. Keep ranking provenance and any future traversal state separate. Do not claim a ranking preview has run downstream profitability judgments.
- If the No1 ranking settings cannot be read, report the explicit failure; never silently replace the selected category with all categories. No1 operations remain background-only.
- After matching the ranking candidate to KREAM and its source option, use `cream_musinsa_batch_start` within the confirmed N work order. This persists the `musinsa-ranking` analysis profile through launch and resume. Do not route ranking candidates into the general `cream_batch_start` by default.
- Musinsa screening centers on margin rate by sale mode, source option stock, and recent sales volume/price trends. Candidate selection remains deterministic; existing product owner and independent reviewer roles remain. Read the precomputed signals and their saved calculation evidence; do not repeat collection, optimize every coupon scenario or launch additional research agents during this first pass. Unknown option bindings are a short hold, not an invitation to expand research.
- When the user explicitly says to continue until N profitable products are found, use the distinct `cream_musinsa_profit_work_start` scope (`targetProfitableProducts=N`) and `musinsa-profit-runner.mjs`; do not misrecord it as inspecting only N products. Count unique products with an independently reviewed adopt judgment AND matched available stock, positive observed-input margin and recent per-size sales evidence on KREAM or POIZON. POIZON IDs are namespaced (`poizon-<spuId>`); a cross-market packet and its canonical KREAM alias count once. This is a conditional research candidate count, never a purchase or guaranteed profit count.
- The profit runner keeps ranking state in `musinsa-discovery`, separate from general discovery. In all-category scope it reads the overall ranking first, then the fixed subcategory list; a specifically selected category does not expand to other categories. Pending packets, source deduplication and category position must survive an interruption. On source exhaustion report the remaining target; never fabricate matches or lower the checks to reach N.
- As explicitly requested on 2026-11-11, continuous discovery retries a product lookup failure once after one second, then records a failed lookup and moves to the next rank without a work-wide wait. Keep that source in persistent deduplication so later cycles do not repeat it. Failed lookups are not profitability rejections or qualified products. Apply this only to recognized product lookup failures/timeouts; authentication, disconnected transport and invalid data still require work-level recovery. As requested on 2026-11-13 after repeated halts, finite Musinsa inspection/profit work applies the same one-retry skip; a product-specific lookup failure no longer stops the whole work. Finite Musinsa work also runs under the `musinsa-continuous.mjs` supervisor: environment failures (closed No1 web tab, login, transport) wait and retry, and the watchdog wakes it on recovery; it still ends at its N target or on source exhaustion without restarting the ranking cycle.

Trade records with `isImmediateDelivery=true` are ⚡ storage-sale (보관판매) trades and back the fast/보판 mode; records without the flag back the normal/일판 mode.
